# Photo Placement Guide

Place your photograph files in the corresponding folders:

- `assets/images/elements/element-1/` -> Your 1st chosen element photo (e.g., `line.jpg`)
- `assets/images/elements/element-2/` -> Your 2nd chosen element photo (e.g., `texture.jpg`)
- `assets/images/elements/element-3/` -> Your 3rd chosen element photo (e.g., `color.jpg`)
- `assets/images/profile/` -> Your profile / portrait photo (e.g., `profile.jpg`)

Supported formats: `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.webp`.
