### Photo Elements Checklist
Please select 3 elements from below and place your photos in the corresponding folder under `assets/images/elements/`:

1. [ ] **Line** (e.g. leading lines, horizontal/vertical rhythm, dynamic diagonals)
2. [ ] **Shape** (2D silhouettes, geometric forms, organic silhouettes)
3. [ ] **Form** (3D illusion created through light and shadow, depth)
4. [ ] **Color** (color harmonies, contrast, mood, monochromatic or complementary)
5. [ ] **Texture** (tactile details, surfaces, macro textures, weathered patterns)
6. [ ] **Value** (range of tones from pure black to pure white, chiaroscuro, high-key/low-key)
7. [ ] **Space** (positive vs. negative space, framing, depth of field, isolation)
